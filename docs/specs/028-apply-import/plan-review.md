# Plan review: spec 028 (apply an import)

**Plan reviewed:** commit `a13fca5` (`plan.md`, `data-model.md`, `contracts/api.md`, ADR 0022), against spec 1.3.1.
**Reviews:** two independent, read-only reviews of that plan, both **NEEDS REVISION** (0 critical, 7 major each):
- **S** — Sonnet 5.5: 7 major (S-M1 to S-M7), 14 minor (S-m1 to S-m14).
- **O** — Opus: 7 major (O-M1 to O-M7), 13 minor (O-m1 to O-m13).

They overlap heavily. The 41 findings (21 from S, 20 from O) are resolved below in 34 rows: 40 are resolved, one is rejected with a reason (S-m8), and three are adopted in part (O-M7's reordering is not adopted, AC-2.3's concurrency is checked by hand, and the `purge` wording is refined). Spec 028 became **1.3.2** (a PATCH through `sdd-spec-update`) for the clarifications the maintainer ruled. The revised plan has not been reviewed again.

## Maintainer rulings of 2026-10-20 applied

1. Direct uploads and the disk service's `PUT` answer 404 until a feature needs them; ADR 0022 says the first such feature defines the owner rule.
2. Active Storage's routes stay drawn and gated behind sign-in and tenant checks; AC-1.12 stands.
3. ADR 0022 is corrected: the gate authorises original blobs only; the first image feature extends ownership to variant records and preview images. Variant resolution is not implemented.
4. Purge: `has_one_attached :file, dependent: false`; one `ImportUpload#remove!` path; the blob is released after the commit inside `begin/rescue StandardError`; the request still succeeds; the sweep rescues per item.
5. The 4 s bar: a Phase-5 spike; Ruby work out of the transaction; a lean-read fingerprint rework before any maintainer fallback.
6. `created_at` in the fingerprint confirmed; spec 1.3.2.
7. Presence and expiry checked twice; the one inside the transaction is authoritative.
