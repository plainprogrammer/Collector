# Scanner

How a collector adds a card by pointing a phone at it: the one-screen scanner page (its shell, sheets and lines) and its camera, the on-device reading of a card's name bar and collector line, the optional art match, the ranked candidates, adding a copy per finish, Other printings, the sitting that lists what was added with its Undo, the condition and place a sitting adds with, the guide the capture crops, and the development-only measurement mode and findings tooling behind the settings the scanner shipped with. This is the current truth; the specs it distils record what each feature promised when it shipped (see Sources and the [specs index](../specs/README.md)).

## Glossary

- **Reading:** one capture (or one picked photo) and its result: the recognised text, what was parsed, and the ranked candidates. A new capture starts a new reading (spec 009 Terms).
- **Reading key:** an opaque 32-character lowercase hex value the page mints for each reading, from `crypto.getRandomValues`, never derived from the text (spec 009 Terms, AC-1.5; the shape is `Scanner::Sitting::KEY_FORMAT`).
- **Strips:** the name-bar strip and the collector-line strip cut from the card's picture; the only parts of the picture that are read (spec 007 AC-1.3, AC-2.1).
- **Guide:** the card-shaped (63:88) box drawn over the live feed, in the stage's aiming area (the live guide, which grows with the stage); a picked photo's card is placed in the shipped framing's box, by detection or, failing that, by guide placement (spec 007 AC-1.1; spec 009 AC-7.1, AC-7.2; spec 082 decisions 2, 3).
- **Candidate:** a printing offered for a reading, at most 3 (spec 009 Terms). **Printing:** one catalog entry (`Catalog::Entry`) ([catalog core](catalog-core.md)).
- **Evidence:** the named kinds behind a candidate: `collector_line`, `collector_line_corrected`, `name`, `art`, `art_weak` (spec 009 AC-5.5; spec 011 AC-6.6).
- **Strong name match:** the top name candidate whose Jaro-Winkler score against the cleaned query is at least `MTG::Reading::STRONG_NAME_SCORE` (spec 009 AC-5.1).
- **Tier:** the first part of the ranking rule a candidate qualifies on: `art`, `strong_name`, `collector_line`, `art_weak` or `name_rank` (spec 011 Glossary, AC-6.7).
- **Artwork:** one illustration, Scryfall's `illustration_id`; several printings can share one (spec 011 Glossary). **An artwork's printings:** those the scanner ranks over: not retired, of the ordinary card kind, English. **Usable artwork:** one of those sent that is in the artwork table, has printings and has a card (spec 011 Glossary; `MTG::Art::Evidence`).
- **Fingerprint:** the 1,024-bit value of ADR 0006 made from the art box of the card. **Distance:** the Hamming distance between two fingerprints, 0 to 1,024 bits, the smallest over the query's six offsets (spec 011 Glossary).
- **Confident art / weak art:** the nearest usable artwork is at or below the margin / a text candidate's card, other than the confident-art card, owns one of the 10 nearest usable artworks, whatever its distance (spec 011 Glossary; maintainer 2026-10-07).
- **Art index:** one 144-byte record per artwork (id and fingerprint), built by the app and downloaded by the scanner page (spec 011 Glossary, AC-3.9). **Art matching on:** `COLLECTOR_MTG_ART_MATCHING` is set (spec 011 Story 1).
- **Sitting:** the account's run of scanner adds, open until Done, at most one per account (`Scanner::Sitting`). **Sitting entry:** one add recorded in a sitting (`Scanner::SittingEntry`); never a catalog entry (spec 009 Terms, FR-2).
- **The sitting's choices:** its condition (a key of the collecting vocabulary) and its place (a place or a zone of a deck), which every later add takes (spec 042 Terms; spec 040 Terms; spec 065 decision 8). **Place / unplaced:** a place, a zone or none; a deck's own row is never one (spec 040 Terms; spec 065 decision 8). A zone removed with its deck clears the sitting's place as a removed place does (spec 065 AC-5.4).
- **Photo path / live path:** a picked photo / a frame grabbed from the live feed (spec 007 Story 4; spec 009 Story 7).
- **Shell, stage, pane, sheet:** the scanner page's parts as spec 080 drew them: the shell (`c-scan`) is the whole screen, the stage is everything between its top and bottom bars, the pane (`c-scanpane`) is what a result fills the stage with, a sheet is a modal `dialog.c-sheet` over the page (spec 082 Terms).
- **Destination:** the top bar's button showing the sitting's place and condition, which opens the destination sheet. **Main button:** the bottom bar's middle button, "Capture", or "Scan again" while a result shows (spec 082 Terms, decisions 7, 8).
- **Status line, after-add line:** the two lines over the camera (spec 080 decision 17), each a `role="status"` paragraph whose text is replaced in place; the first says what the reader is doing, the second what an add did, with Undo beside it (spec 082 Terms, decisions 10, 12).
- **Frozen frame:** the captured frame drawn on a canvas over the stage while a reading runs and a result shows (spec 082 Terms, decision 11). **Stage notice:** a message centred on the stage when the camera, the engine or the script can't be used (spec 082 decision 14).
- **Full framing / shipped framing:** the live guide in the full-height stage, and the guide of specs 007 to 011 (the stage 3:4, the guide 80% of its height and at most 90% of its width) (spec 082 Terms).
- **Measurement mode:** the development-only recording of captures against a manifest of known cards (spec 007 Story 5, FR-6).

## Invariants

- **Scanning needs a sign-in, apart from two public files.** Every scanner page and request requires a signed-in user, measurement mode's local-only routes aside (development only, below); only the engine's files (`/ocr/v7.0.0/*`) and the art index (`/scanner/art/*`) need none, since they are global files with nothing of an account in them (spec 007 FR-1, AC-1.2; spec 011 AC-4.1, AC-4.4). The scanner is reachable from the "Scan" navigation entry and the collection page's "Scan cards" (spec 009 AC-8.1, AC-8.2).
- **The picture never leaves the device.** In normal use the app receives the two strips' recognised text, the reading key, a live capture's up to 10 nearest artwork ids with their distances, and the add, Undo, Other printings and sitting-choice requests; no frame, strip, photo, outline or fingerprint, and nothing goes to another host (spec 007 FR-3; spec 009 FR-5; spec 011 FR-5, AC-5.5; [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-card-recognition-in-the-browser.md); [ADR 0007](../adr/0007-art-search-in-the-browser.md)). Measurement mode, development only, may send more (below).
- **Recognition, detection and the art search run in the browser, first-party.** Under the scanner's policy no third-party script is loaded, and detection and the art search add no dependency (spec 009 AC-7.5, FR-4; spec 011 FR-4; ADR 0004, ADR 0005).
- **The engine loads only on the scanner.** No other page loads it or sends the scanner's policy (spec 007 AC-2.3, FR-3). The scanner page's own response sends the policy and a per-request nonce for its inline tags (spec 007 AC-2.4; ADR 0001). The policy is `ScannerPage`'s directives, and besides the ones AC-2.4 names they include `object-src 'none'`, `base-uri 'self'`, `form-action 'self'`, `frame-ancestors 'self'`, `font-src 'self'` and, in `img-src`, `data:` and `blob:`, which spec 007's contract lists with the rest of the header but no criterion states: all part of the scanner's policy as decided in [#219](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/219) ruling 1, alongside spec 007 AC-2.4, its [contract](../specs/007-card-scanner-live-capture/contracts/api.md) and ADR 0001. The whole header, every directive and source, is pinned by `spec/requests/footer_spec.rb` ("under the scanner's Content-Security-Policy"), which fails when one is removed or added.
- **Capture and add are the collector's taps.** Nothing captures automatically and nothing is added without a tap on an add button, not even from the foil marker (spec 007 FR-2; spec 009 FR-1).
- **The camera is never kept across visits, and never released within one.** Every track ends when the page is left, cached, hidden or the controller disconnects, and returning asks for the camera again (spec 007 AC-1.4, FR-2). Opening or closing a sheet, a "Set", an add or an Undo never navigates or releases it: the track stays `live`; only "Details", Done and Back leave the page (spec 082 FR-2, FR-4, AC-2.1, AC-6.1).
- **The OCR worker lives for one page load.** The scanner's head sends `turbo-visit-control: reload`, so every visit to the scanner is a full page load, which is what releases the camera; a return therefore creates a fresh Tesseract worker (`recognition.js`'s `loadEngine`), its files coming from the browser's HTTP cache, so no worker is reused across visits to the scanner; a worker is never terminated, so one left behind by a Turbo visit away from the scanner lasts until the next full load (spec 007 FR-2; [#219](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/219) ruling 2).
- **Matching calls no external service.** Reading a card, ranking and the art evidence run on the local catalog and the artwork table (spec 007 FR-4; spec 009 FR-3; spec 011 FR-3).
- **One reading adds at most one copy.** The add carries the reading key and the open sitting holds one entry per key, so a retry, a duplicated request or another button of the same reading changes nothing and is answered with that entry's current state; a second copy needs a second reading (spec 009 AC-1.5, AC-1.7, FR-1; unique index on `scanner_sitting_id, reading_key`).
- **A scanner add is a catalog Add with the sitting's choices.** It merges into the lot of the same identity and keeps the 9,999 cap, and sets no price and no quantity other than 1 (spec 009 AC-1.2, AC-1.6, FR-1; spec 040 AC-1.3; spec 042 AC-1.3; [collection and lots](collection-and-lots.md)). The finish is the one tapped, Nonfoil included; a printing with no finish listed leaves it unspecified (spec 009 AC-1.2).
- **The sitting is tenant-owned working state.** It carries `account_id`, at most one is open per account (unique index), every read and write goes through `Current.account`, another account's entry answers 404, and it survives sign-out and devices until Done; it is not exported (spec 009 FR-2, AC-3.1, AC-3.3, AC-3.8; spec 040 decision 5; spec 042 decision 6).
- **A refused add records nothing.** The entry and the lot change in one transaction, and `Scanner::Sitting.add!` refuses before `Lot.add!` runs (the key, printing and finish checks before its transaction opens; the condition check inside it, which rolls back a sitting that add just opened) when the key is malformed, the printing isn't an active searchable one, the finish isn't one it comes in, or the sitting's condition isn't one its vocabulary knows (spec 009 Error Scenarios; spec 040 FR-2; spec 042 decision 5, AC-4.5).
- **The sitting's code names no collectible.** `Scanner::Sitting`, `Scanner::SittingEntry`, the sitting's partials (the destination, count, sheets and after-add line included, spec 082 AC-6.5) and `ScannersHelper` never contain `mtg`, `scryfall` or `magic` anywhere in a word (`scryfall_id` and `MTGPrinting` count); the scanner's controllers name the type (`spec/scanner_sitting_core_spec.rb`; spec 042 FR-3, decision 5; [#209](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/209)). The collector-line parser, set-code rules, foil marker, one-digit correction and art evidence live in the `MTG::` extension (spec 007 FR-4; spec 009 FR-3; spec 011 FR-3).
- **One rule orders the candidates.** Evidence kinds are named and `Candidate#rank_key` is the only place their order is decided (spec 009 AC-5.5; spec 011 AC-6.6).
- **Art is on the live path only, and only when the instance opts in.** With `COLLECTOR_MTG_ART_MATCHING` off the index URL answers 404, the page has no index URL and no art line, and the ranking and confirm step are spec 009's; with it off nothing is fetched or built (spec 011 AC-1.1, FR-1). A photo capture, or one before the index is ready or after it failed, sends no artworks (AC-5.4).
- **A bad art part never fails a reading.** It is read leniently and dropped whole when malformed; the text ranks alone (spec 011 AC-6.1, FR-3).
- **Every refresh stores each printing's artwork, art matching on or off.** It writes the front face's `illustration_id` (the first face's, else the card's) to `mtg_printings.illustration_id`, indexed, and keeps each face's `small` image address beside `normal` and `large`. The core names neither: it reaches them through the source's two optional hooks, `reapply?`, asked before the skip decision, and `after_refresh(run)`. `reapply?` is true when printings exist and none has an artwork id, so an instance upgrading to artwork ids applies its current bulk file once more even when that version is already applied (about 760 printings legitimately have none, so "any printing without one" isn't the test; spec 011 data model). The migration is reversible, adds the index and backfills nothing (spec 011 AC-2.1–AC-2.4; `MTG::Scryfall::Source`, `MTG::Scryfall::Mapper`, `Catalog::Refresh`). The build that follows a refresh is [catalog operations](catalog-operations.md)'.
- **The build and the page compute the same fingerprint.** Both read one settings file, `config/art_fingerprint.json`, whose digest is stored with every fingerprint, in the index header and on the page; an index whose digest or format version differs from the page's is ignored (spec 011 AC-8.1, AC-5.2; ADR 0006; ADR 0007). The check is the browser's alone: `MTG::Art.page_config` offers the newest file whatever its digest and the route serves it, and an index for an older catalog version passes it, since the header holds no catalog version, so the page keeps using it and newer artworks get no art evidence. The admin catalog page and `catalog:status` say so: out of date for the first, a warning under "Ready." for the second ([catalog operations](catalog-operations.md); spec 053 Problem Statement, AC-4.1, AC-4.2; #176).
- **Measurement mode is development-only.** With `config.x.scanner_measurement` unset (production and test by default) every measurement route answers 404, including the framing form, and the page shows no measurement controls; it stores under the configured run directory (default `~/card-scanner-corpus/runs/live`), which spec 007 requires to be outside the repository and `storage/`; nothing in the code enforces more than that default (spec 007 AC-5.1, AC-5.3, FR-6).
- **Measurement mode answers local requests only, where it skips sign-in.** The strip, photo and replay routes (`Scanner::Measurements::StripsController`, `PhotosController`, `ReplaysController`) skip sign-in, since the desktop replay is driven by a local headless browser that isn't signed in; so, on top of `config.x.scanner_measurement` being set, each answers 404 to a request that isn't from this machine (`MeasurementMode#require_local_request`, `request.local?`). The other measurement routes need a sign-in as the scanner does (spec 007 AC-5.6 and its [contract](../specs/007-card-scanner-live-capture/contracts/api.md), which lists the replay and the strips as local-only and says nothing of the photos; [#218](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/218) ruling 6; `spec/requests/scanner/measurements_spec.rb`).
- **Text alone always works.** With art matching off, no index built, a failed or cut-off download or a mismatched header the scanner reads text only; a failed or interrupted build never removes or corrupts the index in use (spec 011 NFR Reliability, AC-3.9). Losing the camera mid-session leaves the photo picker (spec 007 NFR Reliability).
- **The scanner is one screen that never scrolls, and its main button is always in view.** On a phone the document never scrolls (its scroll height equals its client height) in 390 × 844, 360 × 640 and 360 × 560 portrait viewports, the top bar is at the top, the bottom bar at the bottom and the stage fills the height between them; the main button is wholly inside the viewport in every state of the table below, and a result's content scrolls inside the pane, never the page. From 640px the shell is a centred column at most 480px wide and the scanner's sheets are as wide as it. The bars grow by the safe-area insets and pad by the left and right ones (verified by reading the stylesheet: a headless browser reports no inset) (spec 082 FR-2, decisions 17, 18, AC-1.1, AC-1.2, AC-1.7, AC-1.8, AC-4.3).
- **The scanner's accessibility bars.** State changes (engine loading, ready, reading, results, errors, art status) go through polite live regions; the main button, torch, count, destination, Done, Back, add buttons, Undo and the sheets' controls are at least 44×44 points at every pointer, and `spec/system/small_controls_touch_floor_spec.rb` lists the page and its open sheets (the sheets' selects follow spec 072's own floor: 36px at a fine pointer, 44px at a coarse one, the ruling of the touch-floor commit, #344); nothing scrolls sideways at 360 px; marks and alignment cues use words, not colour alone; motion respects reduced-motion preferences (spec 007, spec 009 and spec 011 NFR Accessibility; spec 082 NFR Accessibility, AC-1.9). A sheet takes focus on opening and returns it to its opener on closing (the dialog's own, with the `sheets` controller moving it to the opener of the same name when an answer replaced the opener); a screen-reader pass over the sheets, an add, an Undo from each place and a refused add is owed to the maintainer (spec 082 NFR Accessibility). The styles are the [design system](../design-system/README.md)'s.
- **Redraws are announced.** Every answer that redraws a part of the page writes a non-empty sentence in one of the page's live regions, named by the table in States and transitions: the status line (`#scanner_status_text`), the after-add line (`#scanner_added_text`), the pane's `#status`, the destination sheet's `#scanner_destination_status` and the list sheet's `#scanner_list_status`. A modal sheet makes the rest of the page inert, so each sheet holds its own region and empties it when it opens and when it closes (an Undo from the after-add line writes into the list sheet's region while the sheet is closed, so closing alone would leave that sentence to show stale; spec 082 decision 10); an empty region stays rendered and takes no space, never `display: none`, since a region removed from the accessibility tree and shown in the same moment it is filled is not announced (spec 080 decision 8); a region keeps its element and has its text replaced, never a new element; a sentence meant for the pane is written after the pane shows, since a region filled while hidden is not announced. Focus moves only after a collector's own "Set", Undo or add (spec 047 FR-1–FR-3, as superseded by spec 082 decision 10 for where the sentence goes; spec 082 decisions 10, 15; spec 080 decisions 8, 17).
- **The scanner page has no footer.** Its content notice is in the foot of each sheet, and in the `<noscript>` stage notice with script off; the page has no `contentinfo` landmark and no navigation (spec 082 decisions 16, 24; supersedes spec 018 FR-2 for this page, see [catalog core](catalog-core.md)).
- **Measurement mode's framing is the session's.** The framing (`full` or `shipped`) is chosen by a development-only form and kept in the signed-in session, so every measurement write (a capture, a Skip, an event) and the reading's measurement record reach the run of that framing; it is read only while measurement mode is on, and the scanner's own page draws the full framing whatever the session holds. Each framing stores its run under `<run dir>/<framing>` (spec 082 decision 5, FR-3, AC-7.2).

## States and transitions

### The page

The scanner is a focused page: `scanners/show` asks the layout for one (`content_for :focused`), and the layout then gives the `<body>` `c-shell--focused` beside `c-shell--untabbed` and draws no app header, tab bar, `#status` region or footer; every other page's output is unchanged. `<main class="c-scan">` holds a visually hidden `<h1>` "Scan a card" and three bands: the top bar, the stage, the bottom bar. The document never scrolls (spec 082 decision 6, FR-1, AC-1.1; layout in Invariants).

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| Top bar | "Back" (a link to the collection, the back icon); the destination button, "Adding to" over the value; "Done" (the link to the confirm page) only while a sitting is open, else the destination takes its room (`scanners/_sitting_bar`; decision 7, AC-1.3) |
| Stage | The feed (the video and the live guide, shown once the camera is live), the frozen frame's canvas, the status line, the after-add line, the stage notices, the catalog line, and the pane, which holds the pane's `#status`, the send failure and `#scanner_result` in an interim scrolling body, `c-scanpane__results`, until #345 replaces the pane's layout (decisions 11, 14) |
| Bottom bar | The count button ("12 added", accessible name "12 added. Show this sitting's cards."), the main button, and the torch only where the camera's track reports one, named "Torch" with `aria-pressed`; without a torch the main button takes its room (decision 8, AC-1.5) |
| Sheets | The destination sheet and the list sheet, rendered with the page; in measurement mode also the run sheet (below) |

The destination's value is "‹place› · ‹short condition›", "‹place›", "No location · ‹short condition›" or "No location", a zone by its label ("Deck › Zone"), on one line with an ellipsis (`ScannersHelper#scanner_destination`; spec 082 AC-1.4). The sitting's choices are on the page from the first visit and across visits until Done (AC-2.5).

**State is in the markup, never in a script's memory** (spec 082 decision 19). The `scan-shell` controller watches the shell and sets `c-scan--result` while `#scanner_result`, the pane's `#status` or the send failure holds something, so a Turbo Stream that empties them also returns the stage to the camera; it shows the after-add line while its text isn't empty and the status line while it has text and neither the after-add line nor the pane shows, and empties the status line's text whenever it hides, so the next sentence written there is a change a screen reader announces. The main button's label, style and `disabled` come from the same state and the reading state (`card-reader#render`):

| State | Main button |
|---|---|
| Engine loading | "Capture", disabled, `aria-busy` |
| Aiming (engine ready, camera live) | "Capture", enabled, primary |
| Reading | "Capture", disabled |
| A result shows (a capture's or a photo's) | "Scan again", enabled (disabled while a reading runs), secondary, same place and size |
| After an add | "Capture", enabled, and focused |
| Camera unavailable, engine failed | "Capture", disabled |

(spec 082 AC-1.2, AC-1.6, decisions 8, 13).

- **Engine:** loading (main button and pickers disabled, the status line says "Loading the scanner…") → ready ("Ready.", plus "Line the card up with the guide, then capture." with a live camera) or failed (a stage notice, "The scanner couldn't load…", a "Load the scanner again" button; the photo picker can't help either, so "Use a photo" is not offered) (spec 007 AC-2.5, Error Scenarios; spec 082 decision 14).
- **Camera:** starts on connect; live (the feed and guide shown, the torch only when the track reports one) or unavailable with a reason that names the cause (`insecure`, `denied`, `no-camera`, `failed`, `lost`), shown as a stage notice with "Try the camera again" except when the cause is the insecure context, and "Use a photo" (`card-reader`'s `REASONS`). An insecure context never requests the camera. The sheets still open, and "Use a photo" is also in the destination sheet (spec 007 AC-1.1, AC-1.5, AC-1.6, AC-4.1, AC-4.3; spec 082 decision 14, Error Scenarios).
- **Busy:** one reading at a time; the main button stays disabled while the engine loads or a reading runs, and the pickers likewise (spec 007 AC-2.5, AC-2.6).
- **Stage notices** are centred on the stage, on `surface-raised` with a `line-strong` border, for the camera unavailable, the engine failed and script off (the `<noscript>` sentence, with the content notice's lines under it). A broken measurement manifest shows the top bar and a stage notice with the problem sentence and no camera (spec 082 decisions 14, 16, 23).
- **The catalog line:** with the catalog not loaded, today's `catalog/not_loaded` sentence sits at the stage's foot in the alert style for as long as it is unloaded, with no dismissal. It is no stage notice: capture stays enabled and a reading says in the pane that the catalog isn't ready (spec 082 decision 14, Error Scenarios).
- **The frozen frame:** a capture draws its frame on a canvas over the stage, cropped as the video is (`object-fit: cover`); the video keeps playing and the track stays live. It stays while the reading runs and while a result shows, and goes when the result goes (an add, "Scan again") or when a reading throws. Nothing is sent: the frame is drawn in the page (spec 082 decision 11, AC-4.1, FR-5).
- **Scan again:** while a result shows, the main button reads "Scan again" and discards the reading: it drops the reading and its key (nothing about it is sent), empties `#scanner_result`, the send failure and the pane's `#status`, hides the frozen frame and says "Ready. Line the card up with the guide, then capture." The stage is then the live camera, or the stage notice when the camera is unavailable (a photo's result, AC-4.9). Spec 082 builds it because with the result filling the stage the collector can't aim, and only an add would bring the camera back; #345 keeps it (spec 082 decision 13, AC-4.4, AC-4.9).
- **The after-add line:** an add shows "Added Lightning Bolt MOM · 123, foil" ("Added Lightning Bolt MOM · 123" for a printing with no finish; `ScannersHelper#scanner_added`) with Undo for that entry beside it, outside the live region. It names no condition or place, since the top bar shows them. The next capture, or a page load, empties it; an Undo from the line changes the text to "Removed 1 × ‹name› (‹SET› · ‹number›, ‹finish›) from your collection." and removes its Undo (spec 082 decision 12, AC-5.1–AC-5.5).
- **The flash line:** a page load shows on the after-add line either the summary "Added N cards in this sitting." with "Open your collection" beside it (the redirect after Done carries it in the flash, so a reload shows none), or the flash of a page the scanner was left for (Edit copy, a removal) with its Undo; an alert flash is drawn in the alert style (`ScannersController#scanner_line`; spec 082 decision 10, AC-5.6, AC-6.4).

**Where each answer is said** (spec 082 decision 10). Five regions, each a polite `role="status"` element kept in the document: the status line, the after-add line, the pane's `#status`, and one in each sheet. A sheet empties its region when it opens and when it closes (the `sheets` controller), an empty region stays rendered with no margin or gap (`:empty` rules in `additions.css`, spec 080 decision 8), and the pane's `#status` is emptied whenever the pane closes, so no sentence shows stale.

A sentence for the pane is written once the pane shows (`card-reader#paneSay`, the next frame after `c-scan--result` is set), and one for a reading the collector has since discarded ("Scan again", an add, a new capture) is dropped, so a late write can never reopen the pane. Of the regions an answer writes, only one is in a shown, non-inert place, so each sentence is said once; the examples check the text and the element, and that it is said once is the screen-reader pass owed to the maintainer (spec 082 decision 10, NFR Accessibility).

**The stream targets** (the contract between the answers and the markup; spec 082 decisions 10, 20): `scanner_sitting_bar` (destination and Done), `scanner_count`, `scanner_picker` (the destination sheet's form), `scanner_destination_status`, `scanner-sitting-heading` (the list sheet's title), `scanner_list_status`, `scanner_sitting` (its entries), `scanner_status_text`, `scanner_added_text`, `scanner_added_action`, `status` (the pane's region) and `scanner_result`. The routes, parameters and Turbo Stream answers are the ones before spec 082, with these targets added (`ScannerSitting#sitting_redraw`, `picker_redraw`, `say_in`, `added_line`).

**The sheets** (spec 082 decisions 9, 15, 16). Each is a `dialog.c-sheet` opened with `showModal()` by the `sheets` controller, closed by Close, Escape or a click whose target is the dialog itself (its backdrop), and never navigates; opening one requests nothing. An answer replaces a sheet's body and title, never the `<dialog>`, so an open sheet stays open. Each ends with the content notice's foot (`layouts/_notice_foot`, whose lines are `layouts/_notice_lines`, shared with the footer).

- **The destination sheet, "Adding to":** its status region, the "This sitting's cards" form (Condition, Location while the account has a place or a zone, "Set"), then "Use a photo" with the photo hint ("Take the whole card, upright, filling most of the photo as the live guide does, on a plain background."), then, with art matching on, the art line (spec 082 AC-2.2). Picking a photo closes the sheet and reads the photo.
- **The list sheet:** its title is the sitting's heading ("This sitting: 12 cards, Near mint, in Blue binder", focusable, `tabindex="-1"`; "This sitting: 0 cards" with no sitting), its status region, then the entries or the empty-state sentences. Neither sheet holds Done: the top bar does.

### A reading

1. **Capture** (live: the frame at the camera's full delivered resolution, drawn on the frozen frame's canvas, and the live guide's box mapped into it with `object-fit: cover`, one computation shared by the page and the crop) or **pick** (the photo with its orientation applied, `findCard`; found: the straightened picture in the guide's box and the detected strips; not found: guide placement and the notice "No card edge was found…" with the framing advice) (spec 007 AC-1.3, AC-4.2; spec 009 AC-7.1–AC-7.4, AC-7.6; spec 082 decisions 2, 3, 11, FR-3). A photo is always placed in the shipped framing, whatever the stage.
2. A live capture, with the art index ready, is fingerprinted at six offsets and the 10 nearest artworks searched, before the strips are read (spec 011 AC-5.3).
3. **Read:** both strips are read on the device, one after the other (spec 007 AC-2.1).
4. **Send:** `POST /scanner/readings` with the two texts, the key and the artworks; the Turbo Stream answer replaces `#scanner_result`, and the pane opens over the frozen frame. A network error, a server error or an expired session shows the recognised text in the pane with "Send again" or "Sign in", and "Scan again" discards it, and sending again never adds anything (spec 007 Error Scenarios; spec 009 AC-1.5).
5. **Answer:** a text over `MTG::Reading::MAX_TEXT_LENGTH` characters or a key outside `Scanner::Sitting::KEY_FORMAT` answers 422 with a message in `#scanner_result` and nothing is matched; otherwise the page shows "What the scanner read" and "Candidates" (spec 007 Error Scenarios, NFR Security). With the name index empty, or the catalog never refreshed, it says the card catalog isn't ready yet (spec 007 AC-3.8); with nothing read and no candidate, "Nothing could be read…", except that a reading with confident art and no text shows the art candidate (spec 011 AC-6.3, AC-7.1).

"What the scanner read" lists the name text, the collector-line text, "Nothing read" for a blank text, the parsed set, number and language ("Not found" for each missing), "Printing" (`Matched one printing`, `No printing`, `Several printings` or `Not read`) and, only when artworks were sent, "Artwork" (`Matched`, `Looks similar` or `No match`) (spec 007 AC-3.1–AC-3.3; spec 011 AC-7.1). When text was read and no card matches, the candidates section says "No card matches what was read. Line the card up with the guide and capture it again." The page sends nothing about the read text anywhere but this answer; nothing is stored about a reading (spec 009 FR-2 must-not).

### A candidate

Each candidate is a tile (image, name, `SET · number`, set name) and, in this order:

| Mark | When |
|---|---|
| Badge "Matched by its collector line" (or "… one digit corrected") | the candidate carries collector-line evidence |
| Badge "Matched by its artwork" | it carries art evidence and its artwork belongs to one printing |
| Badge "Printing not confirmed" | neither (a guess at the printing) |
| Line "Matched by its artwork" | art evidence, and the collector line matched too or the artwork has several printings |
| Line "Artwork looks similar" | weak art evidence |
| Line "Matched by its name" | name evidence |

(spec 009 AC-2.1, AC-5.1; spec 011 AC-7.2, AC-7.3; `scanners/_candidate`). Then one add button per finish the printing comes in, labelled with the collectible's finish names in its order (MTG: Nonfoil, Foil, Etched); a printing with one finish, or none listed, shows a single "Add". When the collector line's separator read as the foil marker, the Foil button reads "Foil · read from the card" and nothing else changes; its accessible name is always "Add ‹card› ‹SET · number› ‹finish›" (spec 009 AC-1.1, AC-6.5). Then "Other printings". When confident art overruled what the text suggested, one sentence above the candidates names what it overruled, "The artwork matches a different card|printing from the one the name|collector line suggests. The artwork's match is first." (spec 011 AC-7.4).

### Other printings (`GET /scanner/printings`)

A Turbo Frame inside the reading, so the camera stays on: the card's English printings that aren't retired, the first `Scanner::OtherPrintings::SHOWN` with "Show N more" for the rest, each with set name, `SET · number`, release date ("Release date unknown") and its own add buttons for the same reading key. Order: the printings sharing a confident candidate's artwork first (when the link carries a valid artwork id; an invalid or unknown one is ignored); then the read set and number together, the read set alone, the read number alone, and the rest; each group newest first (release date, set code, collector number, language). Opening it is announced in the pane's `#status` ("Other printings of ‹card› are below.", the wording unchanged by spec 082). An unknown card or a malformed key answers 404 (spec 009's [contract](../specs/009-card-scanner-confirm-flow/contracts/api.md), `GET /scanner/printings`, no criterion; spec 009 AC-2.2–AC-2.6; spec 011 AC-7.5).

### The sitting

| State | What the collector sees |
|---|---|
| None open | The top bar reads "No location" with no Done, the count "0 added", and the list sheet "This sitting: 0 cards". The destination sheet holds the control: a "This sitting's cards" fieldset with a Condition select ("Not specified" first, then the scale as "Near mint (NM)"…), a Location select ("No location" first, then "Places" in the locations page's order and "Decks" with each deck's zones as "Deck › Zone", flat while the account has no deck; one `location_options` over `Location::Choices`, like every picker) only while the account has a place or a zone, and "Set" (spec 065 AC-5.1, decisions 2, 3; spec 042 AC-1.1, AC-3.1; spec 009 AC-3.7; spec 082 decisions 7, 9, AC-6.3) |
| Open | The top bar shows the destination and "Done", the bottom bar the count of kept entries. The list sheet's title is the heading "This sitting: N cards" with the choices that are set (", Near mint", ", in Blue binder") and its body the entries not undone, newest first: the 10 newest, the rest behind "Show all N" (spec 009 AC-3.2, as moved by spec 082 decisions 7, 9; spec 042 decision 3) |
| Open, no entries | "Cards you scan now: Near mint, in Blue binder." naming only what is set (a sitting with no entries at all), in the list sheet; "Everything added in this sitting was undone." whenever entries existed and all were undone, whatever is set; "Nothing added yet." with neither choice set (spec 042 decision 3) |
| Ended | The response to Done shows the summary "Added N cards in this sitting." with "Open your collection" on the after-add line, in place of the list, and the top bar on "No location" with the control on "Not specified" and "No location" in the destination sheet; it isn't stored, so a reload shows none of it (spec 009 AC-3.5, AC-3.7, as moved by spec 082 AC-5.6; spec 042 AC-5.2) |

Opening: an add opens the sitting (`find_or_create_by` on the account, backed by the unique index), and so does "Set" with a condition or a place chosen. A "Set" that leaves a sitting with no kept entry, no condition and no place deletes it, the check and the delete in one transaction; a sitting with no entries at all, no condition and no place is dropped when the scanner next renders, which is what a removed location leaves of one opened only by choosing it (spec 040 AC-1.8, AC-3.2; spec 042 decision 4, AC-3.3). A location removed while a sitting names it becomes "no place" through the foreign key's `on delete set null`, and the next add is unplaced (spec 040 decision 7, AC-3.2).

**An entry** is `added` (its lot exists), `undone` (`undone_at` set; it leaves the list and the count) or `changed` (its `lot_id` is null: the lot was removed or merged away by an edit, so the entry shows "Changed in your collection", with no Undo and no Details link) (spec 009 AC-3.6; `Scanner::SittingEntry#state`). Each entry line reads `SET · number · finish · NM · in Blue binder · Added 2 minutes ago` from the entry's lot as it is now, with "—" for an unspecified finish (spec 009 AC-3.2; spec 040 decision 4; spec 042 decision 3). **Details** opens the copy's Edit copy page and returns to the scanner (spec 009 AC-3.4).

**Undo** (`POST /scanner/sitting/entries/:id/undo`) removes one copy from the entry's lot, even if the lot was edited since, and the lot when it was the last copy, ends the session's pending bulk-removal undo when the lot went (`BulkRemoval.supersede!`), marks the entry undone and says "Removed 1 × ‹name› (‹SET› · ‹number›, ‹finish›) from your collection." in the list sheet's region and on the after-add line, which loses its Undo; the list sheet, top bar and count are redrawn An entry already undone, or whose lot is gone, answers 422 and says so in the same two places; another account's entry or one of an ended sitting answers 404 (spec 009 AC-4.1–AC-4.4, AC-3.8; spec 042 decision 3 leaves the condition out of this sentence).

**Done** (the top bar's link to `GET /scanner/sitting/ending/new`, then `POST`) asks for a confirmation; confirming deletes the sitting and its entries, the copies stay, and the response is the summary above, on the after-add line (spec 009 AC-3.5; spec 082 AC-5.6). With no sitting open, Done redirects to the scanner.

**"Set"** (`PATCH /scanner/sitting`) takes `sitting[condition]` and `sitting[location_id]`; an absent key keeps that choice, a present empty value clears it, and neither key answers 400. A condition that isn't a key of the vocabulary answers 422 "That isn't a known condition. Choose again."; a location that isn't a place or a zone of the account's (removed, another account's, a deck's own row, not a number) answers 422 "That location isn't one of yours any more. Choose again." (spec 065 AC-5.3, decision 8); both values are checked before anything is written, and the condition's message wins when both fail. A change applies to later adds only: the entries already in the sitting keep their lots, count and Undo (spec 042 decisions 7–9; spec 040 decision 6, AC-4.1). The answer, in the destination sheet's region (the sheet stays open, its form and the top bar redrawn), says what later scans get: "Cards you scan now: Near mint, in Blue binder." or "…, in Izzet Tempo › Main." (the place's label; the condition alone for an account with no place and no zone; "condition not specified" and "no location" for what is unset; spec 065 AC-5.2, decision 9), including when "Set" deleted the sitting or cleared both choices with none open (spec 042 decision 3; spec 047 decision 1).

**An add** (`POST /scanner/sitting/entries`, params `entry[printing]` (the printing's external key), `entry[finish]`, `entry[reading_key]`): on success the answer clears `#scanner_result`, the pane's `#status` and the status line, shows "Added ‹name› ‹SET› · ‹number›, ‹finish›" with Undo on the after-add line (the finish only when set; no condition and no place, which the top bar shows) and redraws the top bar, count and list sheet; the camera is live again with the main button focused (spec 082 decisions 10, 12; it supersedes spec 009 AC-1.3's, spec 040's and spec 042 AC-1.4's announced sentence "Added 1 × ‹name› (‹SET› · ‹number›, ‹finish›, ‹NM›) in ‹place› to your collection."; a zone is still a label, "Deck › Zone", spec 065 decision 9). A replay of an undone entry says "You added this card, then undid it. Scan it again to add it."; one of a changed entry says "You added this card, and it has since changed in your collection." and adds no button; both go to the pane's `#status`, which stays open (spec 082 AC-4.8). A printing that is retired, isn't in the catalog, a finish it doesn't come in, a malformed key, or a condition its vocabulary doesn't know answers 422 "That card couldn't be added. Scan it again." with nothing recorded; a full lot answers 422 with the catalog's lot-full message; each is said in the pane's `#status` and the result stays (spec 082 AC-4.7) (spec 009 AC-1.3, AC-1.5, AC-1.6, Error Scenarios; spec 040 AC-1.3, AC-4.3; spec 042 AC-1.3, AC-4.4, AC-4.5). The printing is found by its external key alone (an accepted limit, [#204](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/204) ruling 1).

**Focus.** "Set" (accepted or refused) names its own "Set" button as the focus target. Undo (done or refused) names "heading": the list sheet's title (`tabindex="-1"`) while that sheet is open, else the main button, which is where an Undo from the after-add line leaves it. An add names "main", since its button is gone with the result. The redrawn part carries the target as a `data-sitting-focus-to-value` the `sitting-focus` controller reads once on connect and removes; a page render names none. The list sheet title's focus ring is `:focus`, not `:focus-visible`: Firefox gives programmatic focus none once the focused Undo was replaced by the answer. Without JavaScript the sitting's form works and only the focus is lost (spec 047 decisions 2, 3, AC-1.1–AC-3.2; spec 082 decision 15, AC-2.3, AC-5.1, AC-5.3, AC-6.2; the focus ring is a Ruling of #344's sitting examples).

### Art matching on the page

| State | The page |
|---|---|
| Off, or on with no index built | No index URL and no art line; text only (spec 011 AC-1.1, AC-5.1, Error Scenarios) |
| On, index built | An art line "Loading artwork matching…" in the destination sheet (a polite live region, where a change is not announced while the sheet is closed; spec 082 decision 14 supersedes spec 011 AC-5.2 for it as a live region on the page); after the engine is ready the page downloads the index without delaying the camera or recognition (spec 011 AC-5.1, AC-5.2) |

### The two public files

- **The engine** (`GET /ocr/v7.0.0/*path`, `OcrAssetsController`): only the pinned files (`Collector::OcrEngine::FILES`) under the pinned version answer; any other version or path is 404. They are `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`, and a request with `If-None-Match` or `If-Modified-Since` answers 304 with no body. No sign-in, and no CSRF check, since it is a GET-only public library the page loads as scripts and workers (spec 007 AC-2.2). The files aren't committed: `bin/fetch-ocr-engine` downloads the pinned tarballs, checks each tarball and each file against its SHA-256 and unpacks them into the ignored `vendor/ocr/v7.0.0/`; `bin/setup` and the Dockerfile run it, a spec checks every installed file against its pin, and scanner specs fail until it is installed (ADR 0001, changes from the Proposed text; spec 007 AC-7.3).
- **The art index** (`GET /scanner/art/:name`, `Scanner::ArtIndexesController`): with art matching on and only for the current or previous index file by its exact name (`MTG::Art::Index::NAME`), so no other file can be named; anything else, or art matching off, is 404. It is sent gzip-encoded as `application/octet-stream` whatever the request's `Accept-Encoding`, public and immutable for a year, with a validator, and 304 when it matches (spec 011 AC-4.1–AC-4.4; ADR 0007).

### Measurement mode (development only, `/scanner/measurement`)

The page is the scanner's own shell, with a visually hidden `<h1>` "Measure the scanner" and one more top-bar button reading the next manifest row's file, "‹captured› of ‹rows›" and the framing ("IMG_7001 · 3 of 40 · shipped"), redrawn with every stored capture and skip (`measurement_run_button`). It opens the "Measured run" sheet: the run panel (the counts, the card to capture, Skip, Store again) under its own "Run" heading, then the framing switch, "Full framing" and "Shipped framing", each a non-Turbo form that sets the framing in the session and reloads. A broken manifest shows the top bar and a stage notice with the problem sentence and no camera (spec 082 decisions 5, 23, AC-7.1).

The framing is `full` (the live guide) or `shipped` (the shipped card size in the tall stage, drawn and cropped in the same place; `liveGuideRect(…, "shipped")`); anything else answers 422. It is the session's, so a reload keeps it, and every measurement write and the reading's record go to the run of that framing, `<run dir>/full` or `<run dir>/shipped`, each with its own rows, firsts and retakes. A capture also records its framing, the video's size and the stage's (`EXTRA_FIELDS`). The stored notice shows in the sheet, and the redrawn top-bar button shows the next row and the new count; a store failure shows in the sheet and in the pane's `#status`, which is in view while the capture's result shows (the status line is hidden then) (spec 082 decision 5, patch 1.0.2, AC-7.1, AC-7.2).

Each capture is attributed to the manifest row, never to anything read; the first capture of a row is the measured one and later ones are retakes; a skipped row is recorded as skipped. It stores, outside the repository, the strips' text and images, the recognition time, user agent, capture time, the reading key, the outline, the detector's and art search's timings and, with `COLLECTOR_SCANNER_KEEP_FRAMES=1`, a live capture's full frame; add, Undo and Details events and, per reading, an event with the artworks sent, the first candidate's tier and what art overruled. Responses are `404` when the setting is off, a malformed manifest says so and doesn't start, and the stored strips, the photos and the desktop replay answer only local requests, with the mode on (spec 007 AC-5.1–AC-5.6; [#218](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/218) ruling 6, in Invariants; spec 009 AC-9.2; spec 011 AC-9.3).

The findings tasks take `FRAMING=` (`full` or `shipped`): unset scores `<dir>/full`, where measurement mode now writes; an empty `FRAMING=` scores the configured directory itself, where the runs of specs 007 to 011 are, so a run left in the bare directory needs it. `scanner:paired_findings` scores both runs on the cards both measured, lists the cards one framing got right first and the other didn't and the ones only one framing measured (left out), and applies bar A (`Collector::ScannerFindings::PairedReport`; Named constants, Decisions) (spec 082 AC-7.4; a Ruling of commit `7d36c47e`).

## Decisions not in an ADR

- **The ranking order is the maintainer's.** A strong name match outranks a disagreeing collector-line printing, the collector-line printing second (ruled 2026-10-02, spec 007 research §13); a confident art match outranks both and chooses the printing among those sharing its artwork, even against a collector-line printing of the same card with another artwork; weak art breaks ties only and never adds a card or changes a printing (maintainer, 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-07; spec 009 AC-5.1, AC-5.2; spec 011 AC-6.3–AC-6.6). The order is `Candidate#rank_key`: confident art, strong name, collector line (plain or corrected), weak art, name rank (spec 011 AC-6.6).
- **The one-digit correction.** When a strong name match names another card than the collector line's printing, or the line parses but matches no printing, the top name candidate's one printing in the read set whose number is one digit away (exactly one such printing, else none) (added, dropped or changed, leading zeros ignored, any letters or symbols matching exactly) is shown "matched by its collector line, one digit corrected". It never runs when the collector-line printing ranks first or for a candidate below the top (spec 009 AC-5.3; `MTG::CollectorNumber.one_digit_apart?`).
- **Art decides by words and tier, and its printing rule is ordered.** A confident-art candidate's printing is its only printing; else the collector-line printing when it is among the artwork's (the corrected one included); else the newest in the read set; else the newest, marked "Printing not confirmed". A candidate it chooses carries art evidence, plus name evidence if the name matched, and collector-line evidence only when the line's printing is the one kept; otherwise none, because the line didn't match the printing shown (spec 011 AC-6.4). The image an artwork is fingerprinted from is the front face's `small` image of its oldest English card printing that has one (release date with undated last, then set code, then number), since the bulk file's order isn't kept after a refresh (maintainer, 2026-10-07; spec 011 AC-3.3; ADR 0006).
- **Art is opt-in, by an environment variable, and on the live guide crop only.** The first build costs about 708 MB of downloads and about 2.6 hours (ADR 0006), so the instance chooses it; there is no admin switch for the opt-in (starting a build from `/admin/catalog` is [catalog operations](catalog-operations.md)'s). Detection on live frames isn't built because the shipped detector's separation threshold exceeds a card's share of a live frame (5/35 right artwork first against 33/35 from the guide crop); art on the photo path waits for a measured margin (maintainer, 2026-10-07; spec 011 Non-Goals, Story 5; spec 010 research §8). `config.x.mtg_art_matching` is parsed once after initialisation and tests never read the environment (spec 011 AC-1.2).
- **Detection is on the photo path only.** A hand-written detector, about 5 KB, runs under the unchanged policy; OpenCV.js was rejected (less accurate, about 700 times larger, needs `'unsafe-eval'`) (ADR 0005; spec 009 Story 7). Live capture cuts the strips at the guide and runs no detection (spec 009 AC-7.6). Battle cards and tilts beyond about 6° aren't handled (spec 009 Non-Goals).
- **Recognition is in the browser only,** which rules out a server-side sidecar and server-side search of an art fingerprint (ADR 0004; ADR 0007 rejects a Ruby search at about 2.7 s per scan on the desktop).
- **The sitting's choices live on the sitting, read on the server inside each add,** so the add buttons, the page's scripts and the add request carry nothing new and a reload keeps them; the alternative, carrying them on each add, would need a script to keep streamed add forms in step (spec 040 decision 2; spec 042 decision 4). One control holds the condition and the place and is shown to every collector so the first card of a sitting can have a condition (spec 042 decision 2). The scale is the vocabulary of the collectible the scanner reads, and the model checks a condition against the printing's own vocabulary at an add (spec 042 decision 5).
- **"Set" always says what later scans get,** also when it clears both choices: one rule replaces an exception that announced nothing, with no new copy (maintainer, 2026-11-06, option A; spec 047 decision 1).
- **Specs 040 and 042 built on today's scanner before the capture spikes** (#52–#55) because the control and the add path are server-rendered and independent of capture (maintainer, 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-06; spec 040 decision 1; spec 042 decision 1).
- **The image dependency.** The art build decodes with ImageMagick's command line (`magick`, or `convert`), checked to agree with the browser to 0 bits on 134 artworks, desktop and amd64 image. The arm64 image's decoder is unmeasured; the maintainer kept testing on x86_64 (2026-10-08; ADR 0012; spec 011 AC-8.2, AC-8.3).
- **Camera paths are tested by substituting `getUserMedia` in the page,** in headless Firefox, with a card painted where the strips are cut so real OCR runs; no corpus image is committed, and committed fixtures are text only, keyed by manifest `file` with a `format_version` (ADR 0002; spec 007 AC-6.6, FR-6; spec 009 AC-5.4, AC-9.4; spec 011 AC-9.6).
- **Performance targets are reported, not gated.** The suite asserts behaviour, never time. Phone targets: art fingerprint and search add at most 100 ms (median) to a capture, detection and straightening at most 1 s (median) per picked photo, the cold load grows at most 20 KB compressed over spec 007's; a cold load downloads one core build and a warm load none; the art index loads without delaying the camera or recognition and is downloaded at most once per catalog version and digest per browser. Development-machine targets: an add at most 500 ms (median), Other printings for a card with 100 printings under 300 ms at the 95th percentile, art evidence at most 50 ms (median) of the reading request. The findings report each, a miss is reported and not hidden, and the measured figures are in each spec's `research.md` and in ADR 0005 and ADR 0007 (spec 007 NFR Performance; spec 009 NFR Performance; spec 011 NFR Performance).
- **The live guide grows with the stage, and never below the shipped card size on a portrait stage that has room** (decision 2; rule and numbers in Named constants). Alternatives refused: the shipped card size in the tall stage (decision 4's fallback); 80% of the height alone, which is a smaller card than shipped on every stage shorter than 4/3 of its width; the guide centred in the whole stage, where the lines and corners cover its name and collector strips on short stages; the guide as wide as the stage, with no margin to aim with. A line that wraps to two lines may cross the guide's top edge on a short stage: accepted, since it covers the border, not the card's sides, and the capture is unaffected. In a 1080 × 1920 frame a card filling the live guide at 390 × 844 is about 1,000 px wide against about 825 at the shipped framing; with a 3:4 frame the two are about equal, which is why every measured capture records the frame's and the stage's size (spec 082 decision 2, AC-7.2).
- **The photo path keeps the shipped framing.** A picked photo has no stage, its geometry was tuned on photos, and the detector's strips assume it; `geometry.js` keeps the shipped shares under their own name (spec 082 decision 3, AC-3.3).
- **"Scan again" is built with the page, not left for #345.** With a result filling the stage the collector can't aim, and without the button only an add would bring the camera back; an alternative, a Close button in the pane, is one more control and a second way to do the same once #345 lands. #345 keeps "Scan again", the after-add line and the frozen frame, and removes `c-scanpane__results`, the interim scrolling body that holds today's result partial, candidates grid and Other printings frame in the pane until its layout (the band, the swipe row, the details block, one add per finish) replaces them (spec 082 decisions 11, 13, 25, Non-Goals).
- **Measurement mode is the same page, with its framing in the session,** because a boot-time setting per framing needs a restart per block and a query parameter never reaches the separate writes; the paired run is ABBA over blocks of about 10 cards on one server (spec 082 decisions 4, 5).
- **The sheets' regions, the lines and the pane follow spec 080's patterns, and no ADR records them:** the geometry is a measured setting, recorded here and in Named constants, as the shipped one was (spec 082 decision 21).

- **Spikes live outside the app.** `spikes/card_scanner/` (spec 005's server and camera and OCR pages, `phase2/` for spec 008, `phase3/` for spec 010) is throwaway code outside the app's load path; the settings and fixtures the app uses were frozen at `c68ffbd` (spec 007), `39cdc6e` (spec 008) and `7afed14` (spec 009) (spec 005 Non-Goals; spec 008 Non-Goals; spec 010 Non-Goals).
- **The scanner is HTTPS for the camera.** Browsers allow the live camera only on a secure context, so a self-hoster serves the app over HTTPS and a developer uses `bin/dev-certificate`; without it only the photo picker works. The procedure is in the README (spec 007 AC-7.1, AC-7.2).
- **A name candidate shows its printing in the read set, else its newest English one.** For each name candidate (the top one aside, when the one-digit correction is due) the printing shown is the card's English printing in the set the collector line read, when it has one there, else its newest English printing; a card with no English printing isn't a candidate at all (`MTG::Reading#name_printings`, over English searchable printings only; maintainer, 2026-11-09, [#219](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/219) ruling 3). `spec/models/mtg/reading_spec.rb` ("shows a name candidate's printing in the parsed set, else its newest English printing") pins the read-set preference and the fallback; the English-only part isn't pinned by a spec.
- **Rulings recorded here that no spec states.** The scanner's policy extras ([#219](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/219) ruling 1), the one OCR worker per page load ([#219](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/219) ruling 2) and measurement mode's local-only routes ([#218](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/218) ruling 6) are Invariants above. Spec 007's contract lists the policy's extras, and the replay and the strips as local-only, with no criterion stating them; the photos route's local-only rule is in no spec text; the OCR worker's lifetime and the read-set preference are in none. Shipped specs aren't edited for them (spec 027 D4).

**Accepted limits and open issues:**

- The scanner's add looks a printing up by external key alone (Other printings scopes the card by type, and Details finds a lot by id), the accepted limit of [#204](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/204) ruling 1 for the catalog's lookups; the second collectible type must name the type there too.
- English cards only: no Japanese or other languages, no flavour names, no battle cards (spec 007 Non-Goals; spec 009 Non-Goals). [#55](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/55) is the Japanese spike; [#58](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/58) asks for a language for a sitting.
- Art evidence is unmeasured on unseen cards: the 300-bit margin was derived from the same 35 cards it was measured on, one confident wrong match is known (IMG_6806), and art names the exact printing only when the artwork belongs to one printing ([#52](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/52); spec 011 AC-9.4).
- Detection on live frames, continuous capture and art on the photo path are not built ([#53](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/53), [#54](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/54); spec 011 Out of Scope).
- A photo's detector outline can stop above the bottom edge of a card that fills the photo; the completion setting reduces it, the phone sample is small (10 photos) and below the desktop result (ADR 0005 Consequences).
- The OCR engine's cold download is about 7 MB per device, the art index about 5.7 MB gzip (the figure ADR 0006's and ADR 0007's measurements give) once per catalog change that alters it, and the engine is upgraded by hand (ADR 0001, ADR 0007 Consequences).
- A panel redraw closes the "Show all" `<details>`; keeping it open is not built (spec 047 Non-Goals); the list sheet's redraw does the same.
- A screen-reader pass over Set, Undo and the cleared state is owed to the maintainer (spec 047 NFR Accessibility), and, for the one-screen page, one over the sheets, an add, an Undo from each place and a refused add, checking each sentence is said once (spec 082 NFR Accessibility).
- **Landscape and short stages (accepted limit).** The layout is the same in landscape, a column whose stage is short; where 95% of the aiming area's height binds (a stage less than about 1.12 W + 64 px tall: a small phone with its browser's bars showing, landscape) the live guide is smaller than the shipped one. Hold the phone upright; the page asks for no orientation, since a browser lets a page lock it only in full screen. The bars stay and a result scrolls in the pane (spec 082 decisions 2, 18, Error Scenarios).
- **Open: the paired re-measure has not run.** The live guide's size ships provisional: it needs the maintainer's corpus (about 40 cards, none of spec 007's tuning cards, at least 10 sets, 8 foils, 5 frames with no set code), a phone over HTTPS and the paired run, scored by `scanner:paired_findings` against bar A. The pull request stays a draft until it has run. A miss makes the live guide the shipped card size in the tall stage, with no second sitting (spec 082 decisions 1, 4, AC-7.3–AC-7.5, Story 7).
- The sheets' selects are not in the 44 px list: spec 072 makes them 36 px at a fine pointer, and they meet the floor on a coarse one (a Ruling of #344's touch-floor commit).
