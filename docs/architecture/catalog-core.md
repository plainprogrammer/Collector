# Catalog core

What the catalog is and what a visitor or collector sees of it: the collectible-agnostic records and the extension boundary, the source contract and how the one source maps its data, the collecting vocabulary, the name index, card search, the card page and the printings page, the content notice in every page's footer, how card images fit their slots, and the operator's setting that opens the catalog to people who aren't signed in. This is the current truth; the specs it distils record what each feature promised when it shipped (see Sources and the [specs index](../specs/README.md)).

## Scope

**Covers:**

- The catalog's records: `Catalog::Entry`, `Catalog::Identity`, `Catalog::Set`, and the extension tables of the one type so far, `MTG::Printing` and `MTG::Card` (spec 002 FR-1, FR-2, Story 9, AC-7.4, AC-7.5, AC-7.10; [ADR 0018](../adr/0018-collectible-agnostic-catalog-core.md)).
- The registry and the source contract: `Catalog.sources`, `Catalog.collecting`, `config/initializers/catalog.rb`, the records and errors of `Catalog::Sources` (`app/models/catalog/sources.rb`), and how `MTG::Scryfall::Mapper` turns a Scryfall record into them (spec 002 FR-2, AC-9.2, AC-9.3; ADR 0018).
- The collecting vocabulary: `Catalog.collecting_for` and `MTG::Collecting` (spec 004 FR-6).
- The name index: `Catalog::NameIndex`, `Catalog::NameKey`, `Catalog::Name`, the tables `catalog_names` and `catalog_names_fts` (spec 007 FR-4's name index and name matching, FR-5, AC-3.4, AC-3.5, AC-3.9; spec 009 AC-6.1; [ADR 0003](../adr/0003-card-name-index.md)).
- Search and the catalog's pages: `Catalog::EntriesController` (search and the card page), `Catalog::IdentitiesController` (all printings of a card), `Catalog::Search`, `Catalog::Pagination`, `Catalog::CardOverview` as far as the printings list's catalog rows, `CatalogHelper`, `MTGHelper`, `MTG::ManaCost`, `MTG::Legality`, the `catalog/` views and the `mtg/printings` partials (spec 002 Stories 1–3, 10, FR-7, FR-8, FR-10; spec 004 Story 6 but for owned quantities and add controls, AC-8.1–AC-8.3, AC-8.8, AC-8.10, FR-9, FR-14).
- The footer's content notice and Collector's licence line (spec 084): `Catalog::ContentNotice`, `layouts/_footer`, `layouts/_notice_lines`, and `MTG::Scryfall::Source.content_notice` (spec 018).
- How a card image fits its slot: the "Image fit" block of `app/assets/stylesheets/collector/additions.css` (spec 019).
- The open catalog: `Catalog.public?`, `OpenCatalog`, what a visitor sees, and the hosting terms behind it (spec 023; [research 016](../specs/016-scryfall-hosting-terms/research.md)).

**Neighbours:**

- The refresh, its runs, the Scryfall client's waits and timeouts, the download, the language setting, the art index build, rate-limit retries, the admin catalog and jobs pages and the not-loaded notice are **[catalog operations](catalog-operations.md)** (spec 002 FR-3–FR-6, spec 011 FR-2's build run, specs 015, 024, 039, 043). Which rows a refresh writes, and when, is there; what a row is, and what a page shows of it, is here.
- What a collector holds of a card (owned quantities on tiles, the "Not owned" marker, the card page's stats, "Your copies" and "In decks", the owned counts of "Printings", quick add, `from=collection`) is **[collection and lots](collection-and-lots.md)** (spec 004 AC-6.3, AC-6.6, AC-8.4–AC-8.7, AC-8.9; spec 021). This doc says only when a page leaves those parts out.
- What the scanner does with the name index's candidates and with a collector line (`MTG::Reading`, `MTG::CollectorLine`, `MTG::CollectorNumber`), the artwork table and the art index, are **[scanner](scanner.md)**; so is its use of `Catalog::Entry.printed_as`, which finds the active printings with a set code (lowercased), number and language (spec 007 FR-4) (specs 007, 009, 011; ADRs 0003 for the scanner's use, 0006, 0007, 0012).
- Sessions, `Current`, sign-up, the sign-in rate limit and the admin flag are **[tenancy and accounts](tenancy-and-accounts.md)** (spec 004 FR-2–FR-5). Passwords, the sign-in page and its form are theirs; this doc says only which catalog pages skip the sign-in requirement.
- The patterns the pages are built from (`ItemTile`, `ItemPage`, `FilterBar`, the footer pattern, `VisitorHeader`) are the [design system](../design-system/README.md)'s (`docs/design-system/components/`).
- What an export names about an entry (its type and source id) is **[data portability](data-portability.md)**; passing `COLLECTOR_PUBLIC_CATALOG` through Compose and Kamal is **[release and deploy](release-and-deploy.md)**.
- Where the `MTG` namespace and the source-adapter convention are written down is `.claude/rules/` and `.claude/memory/steering/conventions.md`, which stay their home; this doc links them and doesn't restate them (spec 002 FR-9, AC-9.4; spec 027 D2).

## Glossary

- **Collectible type:** a kind of collectible with a registered source and vocabulary, a string key (`mtg` today) (spec 002 FR-1; ADR 0018). Every catalog row carries `collectible_type`.
- **Source:** a class registered per type in `config/initializers/catalog.rb` that implements `Catalog::Sources` (spec 002 AC-9.3; ADR 0018). `MTG::Scryfall::Source` is the only one.
- **Entry:** one catalogued collectible as the source identifies it (`Catalog::Entry`): for MTG, a **printing**: one card in one set, one language, one collector number (spec 002 FR-1, Users and Context). An entry's localized name is the source's printed name or, for a multi-face printing with none at the top level, its faces' printed names joined with " // ", so a localized search finds multi-face printings (spec 002 FR-2, AC-1.3; `MTG::Scryfall::Mapper`).
- **Identity:** what several entries have in common (`Catalog::Identity`): for MTG, a **card**, all its printings; its `external_key` is Scryfall's `oracle_id` (spec 002 FR-1; data model).
- **Set:** a grouping of entries with a code unique within its type, a name, an optional release date and an optional parent grouping (`Catalog::Set`) (spec 002 FR-1).
- **External key:** the source's stable id for an entry (for MTG, Scryfall's card `id`), an identity (`oracle_id`), unique within the type; the key pages and the export name them by (spec 002 FR-1, AC-3.4).
- **Kind:** an entry's class: `card`, `token`, `emblem`, `art_card` or `other` (spec 002 FR-2, AC-7.10). **Primary kind** is `card` (`Catalog::Entry::PRIMARY_KIND`).
- **Active / retired:** an entry is retired from the time a completely processed source file lacked it, until it reappears; a retired entry keeps its id (spec 002 FR-4, AC-7.4, AC-7.5).
- **Searchable:** active and of the primary kind (`Catalog::Entry.searchable`) (spec 002 FR-7, AC-1.9, AC-1.10).
- **Extension:** the type's own record for an entry (`MTG::Printing`) or identity (`MTG::Card`), keyed by `catalog_entry_id` or `catalog_identity_id`. For MTG, `MTG::Printing` holds rarity, finishes, layout, frame, border, security stamp, variant tags, legalities, marketplace ids, faces (name, printed name, mana cost, type line, rules text, power, toughness, loyalty, defense, artist, image addresses), the Scryfall address and the artwork id the scanner uses; `MTG::Card` holds mana cost, mana value, type line and rules text (a multi-face card's faces joined with " // "), colors, color identity and keywords (spec 002 FR-2, data model; `db/schema.rb`). The mana value is Scryfall's `cmc`, a nullable float (a whole number or a half; null is unknown, not zero), read from the card's own `cmc` or, for a reversible card that has none, its front face's, and never worked out from the mana cost, because X, hybrid, Phyrexian, split and adjusted cards need rules Scryfall has already applied (spec 058 decisions 1, 2). The core reaches it through the source's `.entry_extension_model` and `.identity_extension_model` and `Catalog::Entry.preload_extensions` (spec 002 FR-2; `app/models/catalog/sources.rb`).
- **Vocabulary:** how a type is collected, registered as `Catalog.collecting[type]` (`MTG::Collecting`): its category name, finishes and their order, which finishes are special, and its conditions with labels and short forms. The core stores finish and condition as values it doesn't interpret (spec 004 FR-6).
- **Content digest:** `content_digest`, the SHA-256 of a record's stored attributes (an entry's includes its extension's attributes and its identity's key, so a change to only an MTG attribute still counts as a change), compared to write only what changed (spec 002 FR-4; data model).
- **Name index:** the names each identity is known by, folded to a key and searched by trigram, which the scanner's name matching reads (ADR 0003; spec 007 FR-5).
- **Content notice:** the lines a type's source supplies for every page's footer: plain text with the phrases that link out and their `https` addresses (`Catalog::ContentNotice::Line`) (spec 018 Glossary, FR-1).
- **Visitor / collector:** a visitor has no session; a collector is a signed-in user (`collector?`, `Current.user.present?`) (spec 023 Terms, FR-4).
- **Open catalog:** the instance with `COLLECTOR_PUBLIC_CATALOG` on (spec 023 Terms). **Collection parts:** what a catalog page reads from the account: owned counts, the unowned marker, the add controls, the stats, "Your copies", "In decks" (spec 023 Terms; spec 073).
- **Slot:** the 63:88 box a page reserves for a card image: the tile's (`c-tile__media`), the card page's (`c-item__image`, one per face) and the table thumbnail's (`c-table__thumb`). **Shown whole:** every pixel of the image is painted inside its slot, in its own proportions (spec 019 Terms).

## Invariants

- **Catalog data is global.** No catalog table carries `account_id`; a catalog row changes only through a refresh (spec 002 FR-1 must-not; [ADR 0017](../adr/0017-row-level-tenancy-one-user-per-account.md); ADR 0018; refresh: [catalog operations](catalog-operations.md)).
- **The core names no collectible.** The core's tables and attributes hold nothing MTG-specific; every MTG attribute lives in `mtg_printings` and `mtg_cards`, and all MTG code is under `MTG` (`MTG` is the inflector acronym in `config/initializers/inflections.rb`), the Scryfall adapter under `MTG::Scryfall` (spec 002 AC-9.1, AC-9.2, AC-9.4; ADR 0018). A type reaches the core through its source's hooks and its registered vocabulary, and a view reaches the extension through `render_catalog_extension`, which renders the partial named for the extension's model (`mtg/printings/_head`, `_details`, `_legality`) (spec 002 FR-1, FR-2; ADR 0018). Two automated guards check files: `spec/admin_catalog_core_spec.rb` (the admin catalog code; [catalog operations](catalog-operations.md)) and `spec/footer_core_spec.rb` (the layout, the footer partial and `Catalog::ContentNotice`) (spec 015 AC-5.4; spec 018 AC-2.5). ADR 0018 records the rest as known exceptions: comments in `Catalog::Entry` and `Catalog::Identity`, and the card page's "View on Scryfall" link; the card page's attribution was one until spec 018 moved it to the footer. A second type is the boundary's first real test (ADR 0018 Consequences).
- **Nothing is deleted.** A refresh retires and restores entries; an identity or set with entries can't be deleted (`dependent: :restrict_with_exception`); an entry keeps its internal id when updated or restored, so lots and every other reference survive (spec 002 FR-4 must-not; spec 004 FR-6).
- **Pages never call a source.** Search, the card page and the printings page render from the local database with no outbound request; the visitor's browser loads each image from the source's image host (spec 002 AC-3.6, FR-7, FR-8, FR-10; spec 004 FR-9; spec 023 NFR "External data"; research 016 §4, "No proxying").
- **A page renders a URL only when its host is vouched for.** `catalog_url` returns an image or link address only when it is `https` and its host is in the registered sources' `ALLOWED_HOSTS` (`Catalog.allowed_hosts`; for MTG `scryfall.com`, `cards.scryfall.io`, `svgs.scryfall.io`); otherwise the page shows the missing-image fallback or no link. A source's text (names, rules text) is escaped when rendered (spec 002 NFR Security; spec 004 Error Scenarios "no image").
- **Searchable entries only.** Search, the printings page and the card page's printings list show only active entries of the primary kind, except that the card page's list also shows retired entries the collector owns, tagged "Retired" (spec 002 AC-1.9, AC-1.10, FR-7, FR-10; spec 004 AC-8.7). Tokens, emblems, art cards and others are ingested and classified, and shown nowhere in search (spec 002 AC-7.10, Open Questions).
- **A card and a printing are addressed by their external keys.** `/catalog/entries/:external_key` is an entry's external key and `/catalog/identities/:external_key` an identity's (for MTG, a printing's Scryfall id and a card's `oracle_id`); an unknown key, or an identity with no searchable entry, answers 404; a retired printing's page renders (spec 002 AC-3.3, AC-3.4, AC-10.3, FR-8, FR-10). The lookup doesn't use the collectible type: an accepted limit while MTG is the only type, see Accepted limits ([#204](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/204) ruling 1).
- **A search matches literally and tolerates bad input.** The query is cut to `MAX_QUERY_LENGTH` characters after trimming; a whitespace-only query is blank; `%` and `_` match themselves; matching is a case-insensitive substring of an entry's name or localized name (case-insensitive for ASCII; no other case folding); an unknown set code lists nothing with status 200; a page number that isn't a positive decimal integer shows the first page, and one past the end the last page, with 200 (spec 002 AC-1.2, AC-1.13, AC-1.14, AC-2.4, FR-7; contracts). Only decimal digits count, so `?page=010` is page 10 and `0x10`, `1_0`, `-1`, `0`, an empty value and a value with spaces are page 1; `Catalog::Pagination.for` is the one method, shared by search, the printings page, the collection's grid and table and the admin jobs list ([#204](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/204) ruling 2). The query and set stay in the URL (spec 002 FR-7; spec 004 AC-6.1).
- **A search lists every printing of a card it matches.** A card matches if any of its searchable printings (in the chosen set) matches the query, and its group lists that card's searchable printings in the chosen set, whichever printing matched; a card appears once (spec 002 AC-1.3, AC-1.4, FR-7).
- **No collection part reaches a visitor.** With the catalog open, a visitor's catalog pages carry no owned count, "Not owned" marker, add control, stats, "Your copies", "In decks", account name or avatar, and issue no query against `lots`, `locations`, `decks` or `deck_proxies`; every write still needs sign-in (spec 023 decision 5, FR-3, AC-4.1–AC-4.3; spec 073 AC-3.1). Views decide on collection parts with `collector?`, never with the setting, so a collector sees them setting on or off (spec 023 FR-4, AC-6.1).
- **`Catalog.public?` is the one test of the setting.** It is read once at boot (`config.x.public_catalog`), never from the environment in tests (spec 023 FR-1, AC-1.1, AC-1.2, decision 1).
- **The footer is one place: the types' notices, then the core's licence line.** The layout renders `layouts/_footer` once on every response rendered with the application layout, and it is always there; each notice line comes from a registered source's optional `.content_notice`, the last line is Collector's own licence line, and core code names no collectible, source or rights holder (spec 018 FR-1, FR-2, AC-1.1–AC-1.8, AC-2.5; spec 084 decisions 1 and 2, AC-1.5).
- **A card image is shown whole and in its own proportions** in all three slots, whatever its proportions, and the rule is a block of its own in `additions.css` (spec 019 FR-1–FR-3, Goals; spec 057 decision 12).

## States and transitions

### An entry

- **Active** (`retired_at` empty): shown by search and the printings page when of the primary kind; its page renders normally.
- **Retired:** an entry absent from a completely processed source file gets `retired_at`; it is dropped from search and the printings page, and a card whose printings are all retired doesn't appear. Its card page still renders with 200 and the notice "This printing is no longer present in the upstream source."; an owned retired printing stays in the card page's list, tagged "Retired" (spec 002 AC-1.10, AC-3.3, AC-7.4; spec 004 AC-8.7).
- **Restored:** an entry that reappears in a later file is active again with its id (spec 002 AC-7.5). Entries never go to any other state; nothing deletes them.

### Search (`GET /catalog/entries`)

A signed-in collector, or anyone when the catalog is open (spec 004 FR-2; spec 023). The page has the title "Search", a GET form (a sticky filter bar; the page works without scripting) with the name input (the `/` key focuses it) and a set select ("All sets", then each set with a searchable printing as "Name (CODE)", newest release first, then code), then, in order (spec 002 AC-2.3; spec 004 AC-6.1, AC-6.4, AC-6.7, FR-14):

| Condition | The page shows |
|---|---|
| Always | The line under the filter bar: the count ("12 cards", counting cards, once the query or set is active) and "Catalog updated <date>" for the most recent applied run when there is one (spec 002 AC-1.12; spec 004 AC-6.4) |
| A type has no applied refresh | The not-loaded notice ([catalog operations](catalog-operations.md)) |
| No query and no set | "Type part of a card name to search." and no results (spec 002 AC-1.8; spec 004 AC-6.5) |
| Active and nothing matches | `No cards match "<query>". Check the spelling or try part of the name.` with status 200 (spec 002 AC-1.7; spec 004 AC-6.5) |
| Results | Groups of up to `PER_PAGE` cards, each a section headed by the card name with "N printings" (those passing the set filter) and tiles for its `ENTRIES_PER_GROUP` newest; when a card has more printings than listed, a "Show all N printings" button to the printings page carrying the set filter; then "Previous", "Page X of Y" and "Next" (`rel="prev"`/`"next"`) when there is more than one page (spec 002 AC-1.4, AC-1.4a, AC-1.6, AC-10.2; spec 004 AC-6.2) |

Groups are ordered by card name, then identity id; a group's printings newest release first, then set code, collector number and language (spec 002 AC-1.4, AC-1.6). Collector numbers compare as numbers, see "Collector-number order" below ([#204](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/204) ruling 3). A tile shows the image (or the name and `SET · number` on a plain fallback), the printing's printed name (the localized name for a non-English printing that has one, else its name, because Scryfall's English `printed_name` can be stylized), a `SET · number` tag and the language code, and links to the printing's card page (spec 004 AC-6.2, 2.1.3). What a collector's tile adds is [collection and lots](collection-and-lots.md)'.

### The card page (`GET /catalog/entries/:external_key`)

In order: the breadcrumb (Search or, with the collection mark, Collection; the type's category name; the card's name); for a retired printing the notice; the image of each distinct face (faces sharing an image are named together; no spec source) with the caption "Showing SET · number · LANG" (a face without an image gets its name on the fallback inside its own slot); the card's name as the one title and the category chip; the "More actions" menu with "View on Scryfall" when the printing's `scryfall_uri` passes `catalog_url`; then the extension's partials (`render_catalog_extension`), which for `MTG::Printing` show, for each face, its type line, mana cost and rules text, a details list (set and `SET · number`, language, printed name when the entry has a localized one, rarity as a word, finishes, release date, "Unknown" when it has none (no spec source), artist, one per face and in face order for a multi-face printing), and at the end "Format legality"; for a collector the stats (Owned, In decks, Free to use) and "Your copies"; "Printings", "In decks" (a collection part, [collection and lots](collection-and-lots.md)) before the legality section (spec 073 decision 4; spec 002 AC-3.1, AC-3.2, AC-3.3; spec 004 AC-8.1, AC-8.2, FR-9; spec 019 AC-2.5).

- **Mana cost** is drawn as the app's own coloured pips with letters, one per symbol in printed order, with an accessible label reading the whole cost ("Mana cost: 1 generic, 1 red, 1 red"); hybrid, Phyrexian and snow symbols show their text in a tag and read as "white or blue", "Phyrexian green", "snow"; a multi-face card shows each face's cost with that face (spec 004 AC-8.3; `MTG::ManaCost`).
- **Printings** lists the shown printing (tagged "Shown"), then the printings the collector owns (retired ones included), then up to `Catalog::CardOverview::NEWEST` of the newest searchable printings not yet listed; each other row links to its card page with its set name, language and "Retired" where it is; a "Show all N printings" link goes to the printings page whenever the card has a searchable printing. The count reads "N known", with " · M owned" for a collector (spec 004 AC-8.7; spec 023 decision 5).
- **Format legality** lists Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper, Commander, Oathbreaker and Premodern, in that order (`MTG::Legality::FORMATS`); any other format is left out, and a listed one missing from the data reads "Not legal"; "Legal" has a check, "Not legal", "Banned" and "Restricted" are muted with a cross, never the danger colour (spec 004 AC-8.8).
- On a wide screen the image column stays in view; on a phone the image is centred above the details (spec 004 AC-8.10).

### The printings page (`GET /catalog/identities/:external_key`)

The breadcrumb (Search, linking to a search by the card's name (no spec source), then the card), the card's name and "N printings", a "Show all sets" button when a set filter is active, the same tiles as search in a grid, and a pager at `Catalog::IdentitiesController::PER_PAGE` printings per page, newest first as above. The set filter comes from the search that linked to it (spec 002 AC-10.1–AC-10.3; spec 004 AC-6.6).

### The open catalog: who gets which catalog page

| `COLLECTOR_PUBLIC_CATALOG` | Request | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| off (the default) | A visitor | Redirect to sign in, then back to the page asked for (spec 023 AC-2.1; spec 004 FR-2) |
| off | A collector | The page, as before the setting existed (spec 023 AC-2.2) |
| either | Anyone, on an instance with no users | Redirect to sign-up, the first run (spec 023 AC-2.3) |
| on | A visitor, or someone whose session cookie names no session | The page without collection parts (spec 023 AC-2.4, AC-3.1–AC-3.4) |
| on | A collector | The page with every collection part (spec 023 AC-6.1, AC-6.2) |

The setting opens search, the card page and the printings page, and nothing else: not the collection, lots, quick add, the scanner, More, the admin pages or any write (spec 023 Non-Goals, FR-2, AC-4.3). Its value is `1`, `true`, `yes` or `on` in any case, surrounding spaces ignored; anything else, or nothing, is off (`Catalog.public_in?`; spec 023 decision 1, AC-1.1). What a visitor's page is:

- **Header:** the brand, linked to search and labelled "Collector search"; "Search" as the one nav link, current; a "Sign in" button; no avatar, no "Add items", no admin or sign-out entries. **No tab bar**, so the body takes `c-shell--untabbed` and `c-main` and the footer give up the tab bar's clearance below 640px; the sign-in and sign-up pages, which never had a bar, lose it too. The card page keeps its back arrow. (`layouts/_appbar`, `VisitorHeader.md`; spec 023 decision 3, AC-5.1, AC-5.2, AC-5.7)
- **Content:** plain tiles, the printings count "N known", no muted rows, no "Not owned" saying; the card page's context is always Search, whatever `from` says, and its printings links carry no `from`; the not-loaded notice is its first sentence only (spec 023 decisions 5–7, AC-4.4, AC-5.4).
- **Prefetch and sign-in:** a visitor's catalog pages turn Turbo prefetch off (`catalog/_visitor_head`), because a prefetched page is reused without asking the server. A visitor's GET of a catalog page, never a prefetch and never a collector's, is stored as the page to return to after sign-in, so signing in brings them back to it with its query; a refused page visited later overwrites it, sign-up clears it and still lands in the collection (spec 023 decision 4, AC-3.5, AC-3.7; `OpenCatalog`, `RegistrationsController`).
- **Caching:** responses keep Rails' default `Cache-Control: private`; nothing marks them publicly cacheable, since one URL now serves visitors and collectors (spec 023 NFR Security, AC-4.5).

### The footer

The layout collects the lines of every registered type, types in registration order and each type's lines in the order its source supplies them, drops blank lines and a repeated line (the same text and links) after its first place, and renders them one per paragraph, no heading, in a `footer` landmark after the page's content (spec 018 FR-2, AC-2.1, AC-2.2, AC-2.4). The footer is always there: its last line is Collector's licence line, which follows the types' lines, is never dropped as a repeat of one, and never comes through `.content_notice`, so with no type's notice the footer holds that line alone, and the shell is always `c-shell--footed` (spec 084 decisions 1 and 11, AC-1.5; supersedes spec 018 AC-2.3). The `mtg` source supplies two lines (spec 018 FR-1, AC-1.1, AC-1.2, AC-1.9, AC-1.10):

1. "Collector is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy. Not approved/endorsed by Wizards. Portions of the materials used are property of Wizards of the Coast. ©Wizards of the Coast LLC.", "Fan Content Policy" linking to `https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy`;
2. "Card data and images provided by Scryfall.", "Scryfall" linking to `https://scryfall.com`.

The licence line reads by the build facts known (spec 084 decision 2, AC-2.1–AC-2.4, AC-2.14):

| Build | Line |
|---|---|
| Version and revision known | "Collector 0.3.0 (a9f6de7) is free software under the AGPL-3.0. Get the source code." |
| Version known, no revision | "Collector 0.3.0 is free software under the AGPL-3.0. Get the source code." |
| Revision known, no version (one that fails the reader's rule) | "Collector (unknown version, a9f6de7) is free software under the AGPL-3.0. Get the source code." |
| Neither known (an image built by hand without the facts, the test environment) | "Collector (unknown version) is free software under the AGPL-3.0. Get the source code." |

"AGPL-3.0" links to `https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html` and "source code" to the source address of the running build, and no other part is a link; the links sit on those fixed words and are never found by searching the line's text, so a version containing "AGPL-3.0" or "source code" can't move or drop one; the short SHA is text (spec 084 decision 2, AC-1.4, AC-2.13). The words and links come from `Collector::BuildFacts#licence_parts`, parts in the shape a type's line has; the facts are read once at boot, and their rules are in [release and deploy](release-and-deploy.md) (spec 084 decisions 6 and 10). The core renders the types' lines and the licence line as one set through one partial, `layouts/_notice_lines`, which the footer wraps and which any other place that carries the small print renders: a focused page that is one screen high and has no footer shows the lines in the foot of each of its sheets, so such a sheet's foot ends with the licence line too (spec 084 decision 12; the exception to the always-present footer is spec 082's to record if it merges second).

A line's text and link phrases are plain text and escaped; a phrase must occur exactly once in its line, match case-sensitively and overlap no other, and an address must be an absolute `https` URI, otherwise the line shows as text with no links. A link has no `target` and `rel="noopener"`. The footer is on every page, the scanner's and the sign-in page's included, and absent from responses that aren't a full page (a `Turbo-Frame` request, a Turbo Stream answer, the health check); it holds text and the links only (no image, icon or button), reads no database, and renders under the scanner's Content-Security-Policy with no change to it (spec 018 FR-1, FR-2, FR-4, AC-1.3, AC-1.8, AC-3.5, AC-4.1–AC-4.3, NFR Security). It renders with no JavaScript and is unaffected by Turbo navigation (spec 018 FR-2). It is small print at the page's end, never fixed or sticky, clear of the tab bar below 640px, its text at 4.5:1 contrast or better in both themes and its links the brand colour at the line's own weight with the focus ring (spec 018 FR-4, AC-3.1–AC-3.4, AC-3.6, AC-3.8; `additions.css` "Footer").

### Collector-number order

`Catalog::Entry.newest_first` is the one order of printings (search's groups, the printings page, the card page's printings and copies, the collection's grid, the collection table's default order and its Set sort (`CollectionTable::Sort` repeats the same terms), the scanner's other printings and candidates, the art evidence): release date newest first, then set code, then the collector number, then language. The number compares by the digits it starts with, read as a number, and by its text when those are equal ([#204](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/204) ruling 3; the AC wording "collector number" in spec 002 AC-1.4 and AC-10.1 doesn't say how):

| Numbers in one set | Order |
|---|---|
| `2`, `10`, `12`, `12a`, `100` | `2`, `10`, `12`, `12a`, `100`: by value, so `2` comes before `10` |
| `010`, `10`, `10a` | `010`, `10`, `10a`: equal value, then the text |
| `P2`, `P10`, `★` | after every numbered printing of the set, in text (byte) order: `P10`, `P2`, `★` |

The rule is the SQL order clause in the scope (a `GLOB` test for a leading digit, then `CAST(number AS INTEGER)`), fixed text with no value interpolated, so the scope stays chainable and pages with `limit` and `offset`. Only leading ASCII digits count; a value past SQLite's integer range saturates. The order was already a temporary b-tree sort (no index serves it), so the clause changes no access path.

### The name index

- **What it holds:** one row per identity and distinct folded name: the identity's name, and the names the source adds through `.alternate_names` (MTG: each face of a multi-face printing); the folded `normalized` key is what is compared (ADR 0003, changes from the Proposed text; spec 007 FR-5). Names and queries are folded identically (spec 007 AC-3.4): `Catalog::NameKey` applies NFKC, ligatures, diacritics, case, apostrophes and punctuation as ADR 0003 decides; the exact ligature set (Æ æ Œ œ ß) and the rule that each other run of punctuation or space becomes one space are the code's.
- **Rebuilt in full**, in one transaction, by a refresh that finishes applied, and by one skipped as already applied while the index is empty, so an upgraded instance fills it on its next run (ADR 0003; spec 007 AC-3.9, FR-5). It is global, derived and replaceable at any time. The table and its FTS5 trigram table are created by migration and kept in `schema.rb` (ADR 0003; spec 007 FR-5).
- **Matching** (`Catalog::NameIndex#search`): the text is cleaned (tokens shorter than `MIN_TOKEN` characters dropped from every line, then the longest mostly-alphabetic line, one in which at least half the non-space characters are letters, among the lines at least `LONG_TOKEN_SHARE` of whose characters sit in long tokens; failing that, among every line; failing that, the longest mostly-alphabetic line as read, so "Ox" is still a query) and folded; when the folded key has three characters or more the trigrams are OR-ed, the best `SHORTLIST` by `bm25` are re-ranked by Jaro-Winkler, and each identity appears once with its best name. A folded key of `SHORT_QUERY` characters or fewer also matches names within one edit (and is the only match when it has fewer than three) whose length differs by at most one; text that folds to nothing is matched exactly against the raw name (ADR 0003; spec 007 AC-3.4, AC-3.5; spec 009 AC-6.1).

### Image fit

A card image fits its slot with `object-fit: contain`, centred, at the largest size that fits; any leftover shows the slot's `surface-sunken` ground. The tile's, the card page's and the thumbnail's slots stay 63:88 at their size, border and corner radius (the thumbnail's image box takes the empty slot's height, 63:88 of 24px), and a missing-image fallback fills its own slot (spec 019 FR-1, FR-2, decisions 2–4, AC-1.1–AC-3.4). The export's `components.css` crops with `cover` and stretches the thumbnail, and still carries those rules; the override lives in `additions.css`, in one block headed with spec 019, guarded by `spec/design_system_files_spec.rb` (spec 019 decision 1, FR-3, AC-4.3, AC-4.4; spec 057 decision 12).

## Decisions not in an ADR

- **Search was public, then needed sign-in, and is now the operator's choice.** Spec 002 FR-7 made it public; spec 004 FR-2 required sign-in for search, the card page and the printings page; spec 023 lets an operator open all three with one setting, off by default (spec 002 FR-7; spec 004 FR-2; spec 023 decisions 1, 2; roadmap ruling 30, 2026-10-11). The setting is an environment variable, not an `InstanceSetting` toggle, so a terms condition can't be switched off by accident from a page and is visible in the deployment's configuration (spec 023 decision 1, ruled 2026-10-14).
- **"Show all N printings" opens with search**, because it is a page of search results and the same data (spec 023 decision 2, ruled 2026-10-14).
- **The mana value is Scryfall's number, read without failing.** `MTG::Scryfall::Mapper.mana_value` reads `cmc`, else the front face's, else nothing, and never uses `fetch`: a reversible card has no top-level `cmc`, and a `fetch` would make the record malformed and drop the card from the catalog. It is never worked out from `mana_cost`: X, hybrid, Phyrexian, split and adjusted cards follow rules Scryfall has already applied. The deck shape panel ([#256](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/256)) is its first reader: its curve and average read the column through the printing's identity (`MTG::DeckShape`), halves floor into the column of the whole number below and a card with no value is left out of the curve and the average and noted, never counted as 0 (spec 069 decisions 4–7, AC-2.5; spec 058 decisions 1, 2, Non-Goals).
- **Hosting terms ruling** (maintainer, 2026-10-11, rulings 27–30 of the roadmap's decision log): Scryfall passes the "source terms allow hosting" rule for a free hosted service, and for a paid one only if card data stays reachable without paying; no paid plan is built until Wizards of the Coast's position on charging is settled in writing or by legal advice; any Wizards or Scryfall data is publicly accessible on a hosted instance, search included; the catalog's openness is the operator's setting. Wizards may withdraw permission at any time, a standing risk of the MTG type and a reason the app works from its local copy (research 016 §§2, 3, 5, 7). The gaps the research found are closed by the footer (spec 018, #34), image fit (spec 019, #35) and the open catalog (spec 023, #59).
- **The footer text belongs to the type.** For the types' lines (the licence line is the core's, spec 084 decision 1): the unofficial-content notice and the attribution are supplied by `MTG::Scryfall::Source`, with Wizards' wording and "Collector" as the title and nothing else changed; the core renders what types supply and names none (spec 018 FR-1, Goals). The links name the words only ("Fan Content Policy", "Scryfall"), never "powered by" or "partner", and there is no logo or mark of either, because Scryfall's guidelines forbid implying endorsement and the design system forbids a game's marks as decoration (spec 018 FR-4, AC-3.5; research 016 §2). The footer is on every page, not only where a card shows (spec 018 Non-Goals).
- **Images fit by an override in `additions.css`, and slots keep their shape.** The design system is the project's own, in code (spec 057 decision 12), so the fix is a block of its own in `additions.css` with a pattern doc; the export's `components.css` still carries the crop and the stretch it overrides. Slots stay 63:88 and the leftover shows `surface-sunken` (the design system already does this for comics), instead of sizing the slot from the image, which would make rows uneven and shift the layout as images load. The rounded corners stay, the thumbnail's 2px corner as exported (maintainer, 2026-10-11). A card page's missing-image fallback sits in its own slot, since it covered another face's image (maintainer, 2026-10-11) (spec 019 decisions 1–4, AC-4.1, AC-4.2; the unowned tile's 45% fade, left out of 019, was replaced by spec 021's marker: [collection and lots](collection-and-lots.md)).
- **Collection parts are left out for a visitor, not shown empty,** because "Not owned" said to someone with no collection would be false; the visitor's header is the brand, Search and Sign in, with no tab bar, since a bar with one destination isn't navigation (spec 023 decisions 3, 5; the untabbed clearance rule, maintainer 2026-10-14).
- **Signing in from a catalog page returns to it** through the same session key a refused page uses; a prefetch is recognised as `CollectionsController` recognises one, by the `Sec-Purpose`, `X-Sec-Purpose`, `X-Moz` or `Purpose` header naming it, lifted to `ApplicationController#prefetch?`. The path is `request.fullpath`, set by the server and never read from a parameter, so it can't send anyone off the instance (spec 023 decision 4, NFR Security).
- **The collecting vocabulary lives with the type.** The core stores finish and condition as values it doesn't interpret; the type supplies the valid finishes of a printing (MTG: the printing's), the special finishes (foil, etched) and the condition scale (spec 004 FR-6, v2.0.0). `Catalog.collecting_for` raises for an unknown type (no spec source for the error).
- **The name index is collectible-agnostic catalog data,** keyed by type and fed by each source, in the primary database; the collector-line parser and set-code rules stay in the MTG extension (maintainer ruling in spec 007 v1.1.0; spec 007 FR-4, FR-5). ADR 0003's Proposed `names` table became `catalog_names` and `catalog_names_fts`, keyed by `collectible_type` and `catalog_identity_id`, and its fallbacks were fixed as in Named constants (ADR 0003, "Changes from the Proposed text").
- **The source-adapter convention.** Source adapters implement `Catalog::Sources` and live in their collectible's namespace; the MTG namespace is written `MTG`. Spec 002 FR-9 wrote this into `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/rules/` and the steering files, where it stays (spec 002 FR-9, AC-9.4; spec 027 D2).
- **Specs 002 and 004's search and card-page parts are covered here.** Spec 027 D2 assigned 002 FR-1, FR-2, FR-7–FR-10 to this doc and left spec 004's catalog parts, which [collection and lots](collection-and-lots.md) already names as catalog core's, unassigned; the specs index now lists them under this doc (spec 027 D2, D3; #104).
- **Where the layout-to-kind table comes from: no spec source.** `MTG::Scryfall::Mapper::KINDS` first appears in spec 002's plan, not its spec; a spec's AC names the kinds, not which Scryfall layout is which.
- **Page numbers, collector-number order and key lookups: ruled by the maintainer, 2026-11-09** ([#204](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/204), all as recommended). Page numbers are decimal digits only, and collector numbers compare numerically with the text as the tiebreak, both as in the rules above; spec 002 AC-1.4, AC-1.6, AC-1.14 and AC-10.1 are unchanged, because none of them said how to compare a name or read a number. Names keep SQLite's byte order and lookups by external key ignore the type, both accepted limits below.

**Accepted limits and open issues:**

- **Key lookups name no type** (accepted, [#204](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/204) ruling 1): the card page, the printings page, quick add and the add and edit lot pages find a record by external key alone, so two types sharing a key would collide; with MTG alone nothing can. The second collectible type ([#23](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/23)) must make these lookups and their URLs name the type, and the scanner's add with them; until then a key is global.
- **Names sort in byte order** (accepted, [#204](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/204) ruling 3): groups sort by card name as SQLite compares text (no collation), so "Éclair" and "Zap" order by their bytes, not as a reader of the language would. Only collector numbers get a reading of their own.
- Nothing rate-limits search in the app: each request runs a name match over the whole catalog, limited only by the query's length. An operator who opens the catalog is advised to rate-limit at the reverse proxy (spec 023 Non-Goals, AC-1.4).
- Hybrid, Phyrexian and snow mana symbols show as text tags until they are designed (spec 004 AC-8.3).
- Scryfall's English `printed_name` is a stylization, so English printings show the card's name and only other languages show their printed name (spec 004 2.1.3).
- Flavour names (such as Secret Lair cards) can't be matched until the catalog stores them (ADR 0003 Consequences).
- Spec 002 left out, and no later spec added: a search filter for tokens, emblems and art cards; digital-only printings; Scryfall's `/migrations` and re-keyed printings; Scryfall-style query syntax, fuzzy and accent-insensitive search; a local image cache; price data (spec 002 Out of Scope).
- The card page leaves out an estimated value and wishlist actions until those features exist; the collector's "Free to use", "In decks" and their section are built (spec 073, #50) and are [collection and lots](collection-and-lots.md)'s.
- [#23](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/23): comics, the second type, are the core boundary's first real test; its source research may change the core (ADR 0018 Consequences).
- [#55](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/55): the name index's matching hasn't been tried on Japanese card names; its research is the scanner's.
- [#127](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/127): the phone-width image fit examples in `spec/system/card_image_fit_spec.rb` fail now and then with a bare `WebDriverError`; the behaviour they check is spec 019's.
- [#77](https://github.com/plainprogrammer/Collector/issues/77) is [catalog operations](catalog-operations.md)': it names `Catalog::` classes but concerns the admin catalog page, and spec 055 addressed it (an admin can run a waiting retry now; discarding one stays out of scope).
- The unofficial-content notice is not on the static error pages in `public/`, emails, or any response that isn't a full page; and the card page's "View on Scryfall" link stays core code (spec 018 Non-Goals, Out of Scope; ADR 0018).
- The brand on the sign-in and sign-up pages stays unlinked, so a visitor who opens sign-in goes back with the browser (spec 023 AC-5.6, accepted).
